# Delete your SunMoment data

*SunMoment by Gala Apps · 8 November 2026*

SunMoment has **no account**. Almost everything stays on your own phone, so
in most cases you delete it yourself, at once. This page shows how, per kind
of data, and what is kept and for how long. Full details are in the
[privacy policy](en.md).

## 1. Everything on your phone

Your settings, your town, your roof, your appliances, how you pay for
electricity (contract, prices, cheap hours, feed-in costs), your log of
runs and tips, the list "What did the app do online?", a one-time copy of a
setting or log the app could not read, your trial details and what you paid
are stored only on your phone.

- **Android:** uninstall SunMoment, or choose Settings → Apps → SunMoment →
  Storage → Clear data.
- **iPhone and iPad:** delete the app. An older iCloud backup can still hold
  the app's data; delete that backup in your device's settings (Settings →
  your name → iCloud → Manage storage → Backups).

This deletes it immediately. Nothing is kept.

**Files you made yourself:** a file from Settings → Move to a new phone, or
a month picture you shared, is wherever you put it (for example your mail,
a cloud drive or Files). The app never sends these to us. Delete them there
yourself; we cannot reach them.

## 2. Household sync (only if you linked your household)

The encrypted block with your runs is stored at Bunny.net (in the EU) on
our behalf. Nobody but the linked phones can read it.

- In the app: Settings → Household → **Unlink**. Your block is deleted from
  the server at once.
- Already uninstalled the app without unlinking? A block that is not updated
  for **90 days** is deleted automatically. Want it gone sooner? Email us
  (see section 4).

## 3. Feedback messages you sent us

You sent these yourself, by email or another messaging app. We keep them at
most 12 months after your question is resolved. Ask us to delete them
earlier (see section 4); we do so within one month.

## 4. How to send a deletion request

Email **gala.apps11@gmail.com** with the subject "Delete my data". Tell us which
data (for example "my feedback messages", or "my household block" and the
date you last used the app). We reply within one month. We do not need your
name, only enough to find your message or block.

## 5. What is kept

- **Purchases:** Google (Google Play) or Apple (App Store) handle the
  payment under their own privacy policies; ask them to delete what they
  hold. We receive only order details (order number, country, amount) from
  Google, and sales figures without anything that identifies you from Apple.
  We must keep those for **7 years** (Dutch tax law).
- **Weather requests:** the app sends only a rounded town or a large area to
  Bright Sky or Bunny.net. We do not store these requests and cannot link
  them to you. Bunny.net keeps the technical log of a request in memory for
  at most 20–30 seconds. Bright Sky is an independent service; ask them
  about their own logs (brightsky.dev).
