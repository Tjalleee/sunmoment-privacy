# SunMoment — questions

Short answers to the questions asked most. Is yours not here? In the app: Settings → Help and info → Feedback & help. You can also mail: gala.apps11@gmail.com.

## The notification does not come

- The app only sends a notification on days when it is worth it: a sunny day, or cheap hours. On a grey day it stays quiet. How often you get one is your choice, under Settings → Notifications.
- Check that notifications are on: your phone's Settings → Apps → SunMoment → Notifications.
- Android restricts apps you have not opened for a while. Open SunMoment now and then. Under your phone's Settings → Apps → SunMoment → Battery, choose "Unrestricted".
- Samsung: take SunMoment out of "Sleeping apps". Xiaomi: switch "Autostart" on.
- Does your phone hold apps back? A paused work profile (Android), Focus mode (Android) and Screen Time (iPhone) can hold notifications back. Check there that SunMoment may keep working.
- Did five notifications go by without a tap, and without you opening the app? Then the app goes quiet by itself, so it does not keep disturbing you. The last notification says so. Open the app once and they come back.
- The app shows per day why a notification did or did not come: Settings → Help and info → Feedback & help → Notification not arriving.
- Is your trial over and did you not buy? Then the notifications and the widget stop.

## How does the trial work?

- On Android: the trial lasts at least 21 days. Grey days do not count, so in a grey period it lasts longer, up to 60 days at most.
- On iPhone and iPad: the trial lasts 21 days, from the day you start it yourself.
- You never pay automatically. After the trial you choose whether to buy. You pay once; there is no subscription.

## What does it cost?

- Installing and the trial are free. After that you can buy the full version once. There is no subscription, and you never pay automatically.
- The price is in the app under Settings → SunMoment full version, and in the store. You always see it before you buy.
- Not satisfied? Within 14 days you get your money back. On Android: send us a message through Settings → Help and info → Feedback & help. On iPhone and iPad: ask Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com).
- On Android you can also ask Google Play yourself in the first 48 hours after the purchase (play.google.com, in your order history: "Report a problem"). Google decides then. After 48 hours Google sends you to us: send us a message, and within the 14 days we pay you back.

## Does the other person in my home have to pay too?

- Yes. A purchase is for one phone; someone else in your home buys for their own phone.
- Linking (Settings → Household) shares what you both started and the settings of your home. It does not share the purchase.
- Apple's Family Sharing is off. Google Play's Family Library does not share in-app purchases either.
- So buying it for someone else, as a gift, is not possible. The other person buys it on their own phone.

## What does it save while I still have net metering?

- In the Netherlands net metering ends on 1 January 2027. As long as you net, what you send back is set off against what you take. The time your appliances run then changes little on your bill.
- Do you pay feed-in costs? Then every kWh you use yourself saves you those costs. Enter them under Settings → Power and price.
- From 1 January 2027 power you use yourself is worth more than power you send back. Then the time of day does count.

## Can I use SunMoment for my business?

- SunMoment is made for households. You may use the app for yourself and your household.
- Do you work from home, for example as a freelancer or sole trader? Then that is simply your household: the washing machine and dishwasher at home belong to it.
- The app is not made for business premises, a shop or advice to customers. It counts with the appliances and prices of a household, and the amounts are estimates, not bookkeeping.
- The price includes VAT where it applies. Your proof of purchase is the email from Google Play or Apple.

## What does "on your own sun" mean?

- Your appliance runs on power from your own solar panels or balcony kit, at the moment they make it. So it is not about the weather at your home, but about the power from your own panels.
- You then use that power yourself. Otherwise it goes to the grid, and later you buy power again.
- How much of a run comes from your panels depends on the sun at that moment. For each run it is under "Why?".
- No panels? Then the app is only about the cheap hours.

## How does the app count the saving?

- Each run has three numbers. "Used": what the appliance uses in that run. "From your roof": the part of it that came from your panels at that moment. "Extra because of the time": what you used more from your roof than at an ordinary moment of the day.
- Only that extra counts as saved. In money: the extra kWh × the difference between your power price and what you get for sending power back.
- The sum is in the app: tap the small i next to an amount ("How we count").

## I am too late, or it is a grey day

- Too late for the planned moment? If the app says "Switch it on now and it runs … on sun", it is still worth it. If it says "Too late for a full run on the sun", the moment has passed.
- Forgot to tap? Tap "Started" after all, and then correct the time with "Change time".
- A grey day and the dishes cannot wait? The app names the hours with the most sun today. What can wait, keep for the day the app names.

## What stays free?

- With solar panels: today's overview stays free.
- Without panels, with cheap hours or hourly prices: the line "Cheap today" stays free. With hourly prices also "Cheap tomorrow", once tomorrow's prices are known (usually during the afternoon or evening).
- Without panels and one price all day: everything stays free.
- Buying gives you: your plan with timer settings, notifications, tomorrow and the coming days, the widget, car and boiler.

## I already have an app for my inverter

SunMoment works alongside it, with no login. Your inverter app shows what happened; SunMoment tells you what to do now: when to start your washing machine, dishwasher and dryer, and what to set the timer to.

- Does your inverter app show other kWh than SunMoment? That is right. Your inverter measures what your panels gave. SunMoment estimates ahead, from the weather forecast and your panels.
- Want the estimate to fit better? See "The forecast was wrong".

## Does the app switch my appliances on itself?

- No. SunMoment says when. You switch the appliance on, or you set the timer on the machine.
- The app does not control appliances, smart plugs or batteries. That way it works without an account and with every machine, an old one too.
- Do you have a smart plug or a system of your own at home? Then you can set the time from SunMoment there yourself. That only works with an appliance that starts by itself when it gets power.
- Rather in your calendar? Tap "Another time" next to a run and choose "Add to calendar". No account needed.

## My energy app shows other hours or prices

- Do you have panels? Then SunMoment plans on your own sun first: in sunny hours your own power is cheapest. Your supplier's app only looks at the price. That is why SunMoment can name another hour.
- With hourly prices SunMoment counts with the exchange price. Your supplier adds taxes and its own surcharge. So the amounts in your supplier's app are higher. The cheapest hour is almost always the same hour.
- Want to see in SunMoment what a run costs you? Enter your taxes and surcharge under Settings → Power and price → "Your own price per kWh". That field is there with hourly prices in euros or Danish kroner.
- Tomorrow's prices usually come into SunMoment during the afternoon or evening. Your supplier sometimes has them earlier.
- Does the app have no hourly prices for your place yet? Then the app says so. Look in your supplier's app for the cheap hours.

## I already bought, but the app asks again

- Go to Settings → SunMoment full version and tap "Bought before? Restore your purchase."
- The phone must be signed in with the same Google or Apple account you bought with.
- A purchase is for one account. For someone in your household with their own account: buy on that phone.

## I have a new phone

- On your old phone: Settings → Move to a new phone. You make a file with your settings and your log. Send it to yourself.
- On your new phone: open the file in the same place in the app.
- Three things do not come along, so you do them again: allow notifications, restore your purchase, and link your household. After reading the file in, the app lists them for you.

## Why does the app never plan the dryer at night?

- The app only plans the dryer while you are awake: between 07:00 and 22:00. Do not let a dryer run while you sleep or are out.
- Do you work at night? There is no setting for that. Then start the dryer while you are awake.
- The Dutch fire service says that of all electrical appliances, the dryer is the most likely to cause a fire. Always clean its filters well ([brandweer.nl](https://www.brandweer.nl/onderwerpen/elektrische-apparaten/)).

## Does the app look per hour or per quarter hour?

- With hourly prices the app looks per hour. Where prices are per quarter hour, the average of that hour counts. For a washing machine or dishwasher that runs a few hours, that makes little difference.

## The forecast was wrong

- The app turns the weather forecast into power from your panels. A forecast is never exact: a cloud that moves differently, fog or dust makes a difference.
- Tell the app, and it learns from it. Under Settings → Place and panels → Panels and battery → More details the app sometimes asks: "Was yesterday right?" Choose "Too optimistic", "About right" or "Too pessimistic".
- Know the exact number? Choose More → Your savings → "Is the estimate right? Enter how much your panels made yesterday" and enter the kWh (your inverter's app shows them). The app adjusts its estimate with it; recent days count the most.
- Which weather service the app uses is under Settings → Help and info → Privacy & sources.

## I don't know how many panels I have

- Choose "Don't know" during setup. The app then uses 10 panels (2 for a balcony set) and says the plan is an estimate.
- Found out later? Change it under Settings → Place and panels. The watts per panel are on your quote (for example 430 watts).
- Do the panels belong to your landlord? If their power comes in through your own meter, the app works the same. Ask your landlord how many panels there are. Does their power go to the building's meter, for example for the hallway or the lift? Then you do not use it yourself. Answer "No" to "Do you have solar panels?".

## I'm moving, or my panels or appliances change

- Moving to another place? Choose your new place under Settings → Place and panels. The app then asks "A different roof too?". Choose "Yes, different roof", and the app starts over for your panels.
- Moved within the same place? Then change your panels under Settings → Place and panels: the number, the direction and the tilt. Clear "How much your panels made last year". Fill it in again once you have a whole year with this roof.
- More panels? Change the number. Last year's yield then belongs to your old panels and no longer counts, until you enter a yield for the panels you have now.
- A new washing machine, dishwasher or dryer? Under Settings → Appliances, change how much power one run uses, if you know it. Earlier runs keep counting as they counted then.
- Your log and what you saved are kept.

## How do I set the timer on my machine?

- Every machine does it differently. On some you set the time until it is done, on others the time until it starts or the clock time it ends. The app asks which kind you have and then tells you which setting to choose.
- You can read it again under Settings → Help and info → How do I set the timer?
- No timer? Then the app tells you what time to switch it on yourself.
