# SunMoment — questions

Short answers to the questions asked most. Is yours not here? In the app: Settings → Help and info → Feedback & help. You can also mail: gala.apps11@gmail.com.

## The notification does not come

- The app only sends a notification on days when it is worth it: a sunny day, or cheap hours. On a grey day it stays quiet. How often you get one is your choice, under Settings → Notifications.
- Check that notifications are on: your phone's Settings → Apps → SunMoment → Notifications.
- Android restricts apps you have not opened for a while. Open SunMoment now and then. Under your phone's Settings → Apps → SunMoment → Battery, choose "Unrestricted".
- Samsung: take SunMoment out of "Sleeping apps". Xiaomi: switch "Autostart" on.
- The app shows per day why a notification did or did not come: Settings → Help and info → Feedback & help → Notification not arriving.
- Is your trial over and did you not buy? Then the notifications and the widget stop.

## How does the trial work?

- On Android: the trial lasts at least 21 days. Grey days do not count, so in a grey period it lasts longer, up to 60 days at most.
- On iPhone and iPad: the trial lasts 21 days, from the day you start it yourself.
- You never pay automatically. After the trial you choose whether to buy. You pay once; there is no subscription.

## What does it cost?

- Installing and the trial are free. After that you can buy the full version once. There is no subscription, and you never pay automatically.
- The price is in the app under Settings → SunMoment full version, and in the store. You always see it before you buy.
- Not satisfied? Within 14 days you get your money back. On Android: send us a message through Settings → Help and info → Feedback & help. On iPhone and iPad: ask Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Does the other person in my home have to pay too?

- Yes. A purchase is for one phone; someone else in your home buys for their own phone.
- Linking (Settings → Household) shares what you both started and the settings of your home. It does not share the purchase.
- Apple's Family Sharing is off.

## What does it save while I still have net metering?

- In the Netherlands net metering runs until 1 January 2027. Until then, what you send back is set off against what you take. The time your appliances run then changes little on your bill.
- Do you pay feed-in costs? Then every kWh you use yourself saves you those costs. Enter them under Settings → Power and price.
- From 2027 power you use yourself is worth more than power you send back. Then the time of day does count. You can get used to it now.

## How does the app count the saving?

- Each run has three numbers. "Used": what the appliance uses in that run. "From your roof": the part of it that came from your panels at that moment. "Extra because of the time": what you used more from your roof than at an ordinary moment of the day.
- Only that extra counts as saved. In money: the extra kWh × the difference between your power price and what you get for sending power back.
- The sum is in the app: tap the small i next to an amount ("How we count").

## I am too late, or it is a grey day

- Too late for the planned moment? If the app says "Starting now still gets about … from the sun", it is still worth it. Otherwise the moment has passed.
- Forgot to tap? Tap "Started" after all, and then correct the time with "Change time".
- A grey day and the dishes cannot wait? The app names the hours with the most sun today. What can wait, keep for the day the app names.

## What stays free?

- With solar panels: today's overview stays free.
- Without panels, with fixed cheap hours: the line "Cheap today" stays free.
- Without panels and one price all day: everything stays free.
- Buying gives you: your plan with timer settings, notifications, tomorrow and the coming days, the widget, car and boiler.

## I already bought, but the app asks again

- Go to Settings → SunMoment full version and tap "Bought before? Restore your purchase."
- The phone must be signed in with the same Google or Apple account you bought with.
- A purchase is for one account. For someone in your household with their own account: buy on that phone.

## I have a new phone

- On your old phone: Settings → Move to a new phone. You make a file with your settings and your log. Send it to yourself.
- On your new phone: open the file in the same place in the app.
- Three things do not come along, so you do them again: allow notifications, restore your purchase, and link your household. After reading the file in, the app lists them for you.

## Privacy

No account, no ads. What the app does and does not send is in the [privacy policy](en.md).
